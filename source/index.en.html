02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORKHAMBURG, GERMANY

A designer’s eye.
A sense of purpose.

I’m Anurag, a print and digital media designer in training at Macromedia Hamburg.

My work starts with the fundamentals: typographic hierarchy, proportion, and grid systems. I bring these principles into brand identities, editorial layouts, and digital media.

Years of direct human interaction shape my approach to design. I value clarity, tactile craftsmanship, and deliberate simplicity — creating visual stories that are easy to understand.
